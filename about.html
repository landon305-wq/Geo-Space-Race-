About GEO-SPACERACE

GEO-SPACERACE is a free, independent geography game that turns map trivia into a race to the Moon. It started with a simple question: what if learning the 50 states felt more like a launch countdown than a flash-card quiz?

What the game is

Instead of multiple-choice answers, every question is answered on a real, clickable map. You might be asked to find the states with an NBA team in a certain division, the countries that border a sea, or the states that are home to a famous national park. Getting it right means knowing where places are, not just their names.

There are three ways to play. The Daily Challenge is a 60-second mission that is the same for every player, with harder questions on Wednesdays and world-map questions on Fridays. The Elimination modes are longer missions for the USA, Europe and the whole world, where every correct answer removes a place from the map and moves your rocket closer to the Moon. Practice Mode is for learning the map one place at a time: find every state, European country or world country, or match every capital to its state or country, with one life and no names on the map. The capitals games have their own leaderboards. See How to Play for the full rules.

Where the questions come from

Every question is written by hand. Many questions name their source right in the prompt, such as the National Park Service, a sports league or a government agency, so you can check the answer yourself. Facts that change over time, like sports results or visitor numbers, include the year they are based on.

The game currently includes hundreds of questions: more than 120 for the USA elimination mode, around 200 for Europe, over 450 for the Global mode, and a growing bank of daily missions. New daily missions and special holiday questions are added regularly.

Spotted a mistake?

Geography changes and mistakes happen. If you think an answer is wrong, use the dispute button that appears after an incorrect answer, or email us at geospacerace2026@gmail.com with the question and a source. Corrections are reviewed and fixed as quickly as possible.

Free to play

GEO-SPACERACE is free and doesn't require an account. It is supported by advertising on our guide pages. Read our Privacy Policy to learn what information the game stores and how ads work.
