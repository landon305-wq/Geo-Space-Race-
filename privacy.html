Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information GEO-SPACERACE ("we", "the site") collects when you use geospacerace.com, and how it is used. We try to collect as little as possible.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress in your browser's local storage: your daily streak, best streak, perfect days, personal best scores (including Practice Mode bests) and game settings such as Hard Mode. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Leaderboards

The leaderboards record Hard Mode results in the Elimination modes and Capitals results in Practice Mode. When you play one of these modes, your display name, your results and an anonymous player ID are stored in our leaderboard database, which is hosted by Supabase, and your name and results are shown publicly to other players. Please use a nickname rather than your real full name. To have an entry removed, contact us using the email below.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads on this site.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.

To learn more, read How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Visitors in some regions, including the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, may be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Other services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and map and code libraries from public content delivery networks (unpkg and jsDelivr). Like any website request, these services receive basic technical information such as your IP address and browser type in order to deliver the files. Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Children

GEO-SPACERACE is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information on a leaderboard, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
