How to Play GEO-SPACERACE

GEO-SPACERACE is a free map trivia game. You answer geography questions by clicking places on a real map, and every right answer pushes your rocket closer to the Moon. There are several ways to play: a quick Daily Challenge that is the same for everyone, longer Elimination missions for the USA, Europe and the whole world, Practice Mode, where you learn every state, country and capital one at a time, sports-themed Elimination missions, and Space Race, a typing race to the Moon.

🗓️ The Daily Challenge

Each day there is one new mission, and every player gets the same one. The mission is a single question with several correct answers, such as "Click the states with one of the 10 most-visited national parks." Your job is to find as many of the correct states as you can before your fuel runs out.

  • You have 60 seconds. The clock starts when you press Launch.
  • Click (or tap) a state to answer. Correct states turn green and count toward your score. The intro screen tells you how many correct answers there are, so you know when you have found them all.
  • Wrong answers burn fuel. Every wrong state costs you 5 seconds, so guessing randomly usually hurts more than it helps.
  • One launch per day. When the round ends you see your score, which states you missed, and a map of the full answer.

Special days

  • 💀 Hard Wednesdays bring tougher questions that need more than general knowledge.
  • 🌍 Global Fridays swap the U.S. map for a world map, so the answers are countries instead of states.
  • Holiday missions appear on some dates and are themed around that day, for example a hiking question on National Take a Hike Day.

Streaks

The home screen tracks your streak (days in a row you have played), your best streak and your number of perfect days, where you found every answer. These are saved in your own browser, so use the same device and browser to keep your streak going.

🎯 Elimination Modes

Elimination missions are longer games built around a series of trivia questions. Choose USA, Europe or Global from the mode switch at the top of the menu, then press Initiate Launch.

  1. Read the question. Each question describes a group of places, like "a state containing at least one national park."
  2. Click a place that fits. If you are right, that place is eliminated from the map and your score goes up by one.
  3. Watch the clock. You have 60 seconds for each question.
  4. Protect your lives. In normal mode you have 3 lives. A wrong answer or running out of time costs a life, and the mission ends when you have none left.

As you eliminate more places, your rocket climbs through mission stages on its way to the Moon. In the USA mode the stages unlock at 10, 21, 31, 42 and 52 states. Clear the whole map and you land on the Moon.

Hard Mode

Turn on Hard Mode from the menu if you want a real test: you get just 1 life, so a single mistake ends the mission. Hard Mode has its own personal best, separate from normal mode.

Leaderboards

The leaderboards track Hard Mode results: your best number of places eliminated and how many times you have made it all the way to the Moon. Press View Leaderboard on the menu to see the top players. If you choose a display name, use a nickname rather than your real full name.

📚 Practice Mode

Practice Mode is a separate game for learning the map itself. Press Practice Mode on the home screen, pick a map (USA, Europe or World), then choose what to practice:

  • States / Countries: the game names a place, such as "Find Texas," and you find it on the map. Every state or country comes up once, in a random order.
  • Capitals (Hard): the game names a capital city, such as "Austin," and you click the state or country it belongs to.

The rules

  • One life. A single wrong answer ends the run. The correct place flashes on the map so you can learn it for next time.
  • No names on the map. You have to know where each place is.
  • Tap twice to answer. Your first tap selects a place and turns it yellow. Tap it again to lock it in. This stops one accidental click from ending your run.
  • Tiny places are easier to hit. Hawaii has a circle around it, and Europe's microstates, such as Vatican City and Monaco, appear as small squares.

Get through every place without a mistake for a perfect run: all 50 states, all 47 European countries or all 197 countries of the world. Your best score for each map and mode is saved in your browser.

Capitals leaderboards

Capitals games count for the Practice leaderboards, with a separate board for the USA, Europe and the World. Your best score is ranked, and naming every capital without a mistake counts as a Moon landing. Press Leaderboard on the Practice menu to see the top players.

🚀 Space Race

Press Space Race on the home screen to race other rockets to the Moon. The map lights up a state or country, or you get a clue (a capital, a city, a landmark, a state nickname, or a question with many right answers like "Name a country that uses the euro"), and you type the answer. Every right answer moves your rocket forward, and 15 points land it on the Moon.

  • Spelling is forgiving. Suggestions appear as you type, small typos and sound-alike spellings still count, and nicknames like "UK" or "Ivory Coast" work too.
  • Don't know it? Type idk or skip, or press the SKIP button.
  • Misses cost time. A wrong answer or a skip means an asteroid hit and a 2.5-second stall, and the map shows you the right answer.
  • Points: each right answer is worth 1 point. Once you have 3 or more in a row, every answer adds an extra ½ point. On many-answer questions, a rare answer (like San Marino for the euro) is worth 2 points.
  • Who you race: ghost rockets that replay real players' runs from today, plus computer racers of different speeds, so there is always a race even when nobody else is online.
  • Race real people: press Quick Match to race whoever else is searching (computer racers fill any empty lanes after 15 seconds), or Create Room to get a 4-letter code and a link for your friends. The host picks the questions (USA, World or mixed), the race length (15 or 25 points) and whether computer racers fill empty lanes, then everyone launches together and sees each other's rockets live. Online races don't count for the daily leaderboard.
  • Review your answers: after the race, press Review your answers to see every question, what you typed, whether it was rare, and all the other right answers.
  • One official race a day. Everyone gets the same questions, and your first race of the day goes on the leaderboard and becomes a ghost for other players. After that you can keep racing practice rounds with new questions.

💰 Cash, the Shop and the Hangar

  • Earn cash every race: 1st place gets $250, 2nd $200, 3rd $150, 4th $100, and everyone else (including last place) gets $50. Your first race each day adds a $100 bonus.
  • 🛒 Shop: spend your cash on new rockets, exhaust trails and ⚡ boosts.
  • ⚡ Boosts: press Enter with an empty answer box (or the ⚡ button) to skip a question with no penalty, and it counts as one right answer. You can use up to 3 per race. Boosts are turned off in the official daily race so the leaderboard stays fair.
  • 🏅 Challenges: some rockets and trails can't be bought. Unlock them by winning races, finding rare answers, building long streaks, racing the daily race on different days and more.
  • ★ Legendary rockets: eight animated rockets (Phoenix, Black Hole, Supernova, Thunderbolt and more) for the hardest challenges, like finding 1,000 rare answers, winning 250 races or a 40-answer streak.
  • 🛠️ Hangar: equip the rocket and trail you want to fly. Other players see your rocket in online races.
  • Your cash and rockets are saved on the device you play on.

🏆 Sports Elimination

On the Elimination menu, switch from Geography to Sports, then pick a map. They play just like the regular Elimination modes, with the same timer, lives, Hard Mode and Moon landing, but every question is about one topic:

  • 🏟️ USA Sports Elimination uses the USA map. Questions cover pro teams, NFL divisions, champions, college conferences and famous events. Eliminate all 50 states, Washington, D.C. and Puerto Rico to land on the Moon.
  • ⚽ Europe Soccer Elimination uses the Europe map. Questions cover the World Cup, the Euros, famous leagues and clubs. Eliminate all 47 European countries to land on the Moon.
  • 🌍 Global Sports Elimination uses the world map. Questions cover soccer, the Olympics, rugby, cricket, Formula 1 and more, and all 197 countries are in play.

Each themed mode has its own high score and its own Hard Mode leaderboard.

Tips for a better score

  • Start with the answers you are sure of. In the Daily Challenge a wrong click costs 5 seconds, which is often enough time for two right answers.
  • Think by region. Many questions follow geography: Great Lakes states, Gulf Coast states, Rocky Mountain states. Sweeping one region at a time helps you avoid skipping states. Our guide to U.S. regions is a good place to practice.
  • Use the map, not just your memory. Small states in the Northeast are easy to forget. Zoom in on the map when you are hunting for Rhode Island, Delaware or Connecticut.
  • Learn the classics. State capitals, nicknames and major landmarks come up often. See our capitals and nicknames guides.
  • Use Practice Mode to fill the gaps. If you keep missing the same states or countries, run them in Practice Mode until you can find every one, then try the Capitals version.
  • On a phone, tap carefully near borders and use two fingers to zoom in on crowded areas.
